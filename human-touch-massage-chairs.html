Human Touch Massage Chairs: Models, Prices, Sizes, and Buying Checklist

Prices last checked November 10, 2026. We have not tested these chairs. This page compares published specs and seller policies and makes no medical claims.

What it is

Human Touch is a long-running US massage chair brand (Long Beach, California). Its chairs use a motorized massage mechanism that travels along a track in the backrest, plus heat and, on higher-end models, foot and calf massage. Human Touch's own materials state that no medical claims are warranted or implied.

Who it's for

These chairs suit buyers who:

  • Have a room that accommodates a large chair that reclines
  • Want a manufacturer-backed warranty and a published return window
  • Are weighing a roughly $3,000 to $16,000 purchase (see price notes)

Super Novo 2.0 spec detail (official page)

  • Upright: 63" x 33" x 46". Reclined: 74" x 33" x 32"
  • Seat: 20" wide x 18" deep x 21" high
  • Chair weight: 313 lb. Maximum user weight: 285 lb
  • User height range: 5'0" to 6'9"
  • Rear clearance: 2 inches
  • 38 massage programs; S- and L-track mechanism with 3D and 4D programs; heated foot and calf massager; dual-lumbar heat; JBL speakers
  • Shipping box: 77" x 36" x 35", 370 lb gross

Other models have different dimensions and weight limits. One retailer table lists a 285 lb maximum for the Novo Flex and ROVE, and a 400 lb maximum for the Gravis ZG recliner [Unverified: third-party table]. Always check the specific model's spec sheet.

Delivery

Human Touch states free shipping to the 48 contiguous states, with "To Your Door" delivery to a first-floor entrance. White glove delivery (assembly in the room of your choice, packaging removed) is an extra $499 on the Super Novo 2.0 page. Typical delivery is described as 2 to 3 weeks, longer for remote areas or bad weather. Retailers note massage chairs cannot ship to Alaska or Hawaii.

Warranty

Human Touch lists a 5-year limited warranty on models including the Super Novo 2.0, Novo Flex, and ROVE: 5 years structural, 3 years parts, and 1 year in-home service. An extended warranty is sold on the Super Novo 2.0 page for $499, upgrading to 5 years of in-home service, parts, and structural coverage. Read the full terms on Human Touch's warranty page.

Returns

Per Human Touch's return policy for orders placed on its own site:

  • Returns within 30 days of receiving the product
  • A return authorization number (RMA) is required
  • A 15% restocking and inspection fee applies, and refunds can take 3 to 4 weeks
  • Cancellation before shipping carries no fee. Shipped items can incur return shipping and restocking fees.

Retailer terms differ. Some third-party sellers advertise a "30-day risk-free trial." Those terms belong to the retailer, so read the seller's policy and not Human Touch's.

What to check before buying

  • Measure for the recline. The Super Novo 2.0 needs only 2 inches behind it, but its reclined length is 74".
  • Check user fit: height and weight limits for the exact model.
  • Plan the delivery path: the box is about 6.4 feet long and 370 lb on the Super Novo 2.0, and "To Your Door" ends at the first-floor entrance.
  • Compare the same chair across sellers, including delivery, trial terms, and extended warranty.
  • Budget for the return fee if you are unsure. 15% of $10,999 is about $1,650.
  • Try one in person if you can. Human Touch has a store locator and a Costco roadshow page.
  • Ask your clinician if you have a medical condition, are pregnant, or use implanted devices.
  • HSA/FSA: Human Touch lists some products as eligible. Check your plan.

Bottom line

Price and fit move in steps: ROVE for a smaller, lower-cost chair, Super Novo 2.0 as the mid-range reference with full published specs, and Super Novo X at the top. Confirm the current price, delivery terms, and return fee before you buy.
