Sunlighten Infrared Saunas: Models, Prices, Sizes, and What to Check Before You Buy

Prices last checked November 10, 2026. We have not tested these products. This page compares published specifications and seller information and makes no health claims.

What it is

Sunlighten is a US infrared sauna maker (founded 1999, based in Overland Park, Kansas, per a third-party profile [Unverified]). Infrared saunas heat the body with infrared panels rather than heating the air the way a traditional steam or Finnish sauna does, so the cabin typically runs at a lower air temperature. Sunlighten sells four lines, all using its own "SoloCarbon" heaters.

This page does not evaluate wellness or health claims. Sunlighten markets health benefits; whether a sauna suits you is a question for your clinician.

Who it's for

These are residential cabin saunas for people who:

  • Have a space (indoor, with some models marketed for outdoor use with a cover) and the right electrical supply
  • Want a purchase with a longer warranty than most small appliances
  • Are comfortable spending several thousand dollars, with the portable Solo System as the entry point

How buying works

  • Signature cabins have an add-to-cart checkout on Sunlighten's US shop.
  • mPulse and Amplify pages route to pricing requests and consultants.
  • Financing is advertised through several providers, including Affirm and Klarna on the shop site. Terms vary by provider and approval, so read them before applying.
  • Shipping: Sunlighten's shop advertises free shipping on saunas to the contiguous US, with eligibility terms.
  • HSA/FSA: the shop flags some products as eligible. Check your plan administrator before relying on this.

Warranty

Sunlighten's own warranty page lists, for residential cabin saunas: 7 years on cabinetry and heaters, 3 years on controls, and 1 year on glass doors and audio. Some third-party reviews describe a lifetime heater warranty. That does not match the official page, so rely on the official terms. Commercial terms differ.

What to check before buying

  • Electrical. Amplify and mPulse vary between 120V and 240V by model. One mPulse owner-reviewer reported needing a dedicated 240V 20A circuit [Unverified; model-specific]. Ask Sunlighten for your exact model's requirement and have an electrician look.
  • Floor space and ceiling. The same reviewer reports a 52" x 48" footprint for the Believe and 78.5" height including feet [Unverified]. Confirm dimensions on the model's spec sheet.
  • Delivery path. Measure doorways, stairs, and turns. Assembly is advertised as a magnetic-seal system, but you still need to move large panels.
  • Indoor or outdoor. Outdoor use requires an appropriate wood choice and cover per one reviewer. Confirm in writing so you do not void the warranty.
  • Total price. Ask what is included: delivery, assembly, accessories, any promotional gifts.
  • Returns and cancellations. Sunlighten has a returns and cancellation policy page. We have not read its terms; read them before ordering.
  • Ask about promotions' end dates. The Signature I sale runs through 11/29/26.

Bottom line

Signature is the verified entry point for a cabin; mPulse is the premium tier with unpublished pricing. Verify price, electrical needs, and warranty terms with Sunlighten directly before you commit.
