Trackman Golf Simulator Packages: Room Size, Launch Monitors, and Real Price Differences

Prices and specs last checked November 10, 2026. We have not tested these products. This page compares published specifications and seller information.

What this is

A Trackman golf simulator is a launch monitor (the sensor that measures your ball and club), plus the screen, enclosure, hitting mat, projector, and computer that make up an indoor golf room. Trackman sells two launch monitors for this: the Trackman 4, a portable radar unit, and the Trackman iO, a ceiling-mounted unit built only for indoor use. The Indoor Golf Shop (Celina, Texas) lists itself as a Trackman authorized dealer and sells pre-configured packages around each one.

The main decision is not "Trackman or not." It is which launch monitor fits your room, and whether you also want to take the unit outdoors.

Who it's for

This is a premium purchase. Trackman packages in this range suit buyers who:

  • Have a dedicated room (basement, garage bay, or spare room) rather than a temporary setup
  • Want Trackman's own software ecosystem. The Indoor Golf Shop states that the Trackman 4 runs only on Trackman Performance Studio and does not natively support GSPro or E6 Connect.
  • Understand there are ongoing software costs (below)

If you want to hit shots outdoors on a range with the same unit, the Trackman 4 is the portable one. If the room is the priority, read the room-size section first.

Room size: the biggest difference

Trackman 4 sits behind the golfer and tracks the ball forward, so it needs depth. The shop's published figures for the unit and screen:

  • Unit to ball: 6 ft minimum, 7 ft recommended
  • Ball to screen: 10 ft minimum, 12 ft recommended
  • SIG10 enclosure: 8'4" tall x 10'10" wide x 5' deep

Trackman iO mounts overhead in front of the golfer. Trackman states it has no minimum space requirement beyond room to swing a club, and its blog recommends a ceiling of 10 ft or higher. The Indoor Golf Shop's guidance puts typical iO mounting in the 9'4" to 10' ceiling range. A reviewer-retailer notes these are planning recommendations, not universal installation minimums.

Price differences to understand

  • Launch monitor gap. The iO SIG12 package is listed roughly $10,700 below the Trackman 4 SIG10 package. Part of that is the monitor, and part is the enclosure and mat choices.
  • Software subscription. The Trackman 4 package lists a $1,100 per year subscription (first year included). For the iO, Trackman's site shows $700 per year for the Home tier with the first year included. A retailer lists a Home Complete tier at $1,100 per year [Unverified: third-party listing].
  • Optional hardware warranty. One retailer lists an optional $1,000 per year extended hardware warranty on the iO [Unverified]. The Trackman 4 package page states a 1-year warranty.
  • Installation. The SIG10 enclosure is described as assembling in under an hour with two people. The Indoor Golf Shop's installation pricing for a full room was not found. Another retailer, Indoor Golf Outlet, lists installation at $7,000, but that is a different store and shouldn't be assumed to apply here.

What to check before buying

  • Measure ceiling height first. It is the hardest thing to fix later. Measure to the lowest obstruction, then add your full driver swing.
  • Decide indoor-only or indoor plus range. Only the Trackman 4 travels.
  • Pick the larger room-size figure where sources conflict, or ask the dealer in writing.
  • Ask exactly what is in the box: projector, computer, calibration accessories, software tier.
  • Budget the recurring cost: first year is included, then $700 to $1,100 per year depending on model and tier.
  • Read the return terms. The shop states 30-day returns where you pay return shipping and a restocking fee. Free shipping to the lower 48 (exclusions apply) and a 7 to 10 business day ship window are listed.
  • Ask about financing (offered) and price matching (stated, conditions apply).

Bottom line

For a dedicated, low-ceiling or shallow room, the iO is the lower-cost, smaller-footprint Trackman route. The Trackman 4 costs more and needs more depth, but works outdoors. Prices move often, so verify at checkout.
