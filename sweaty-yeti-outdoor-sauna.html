Sweaty Yeti Outdoor Sauna & Cold Plunge: Models, Prices, and Buying Guide

A Sweaty Yeti outdoor sauna is a large, custom-built home wellness installation, not a plug-and-play product. The lineup combines traditional wood-fired and electric saunas with optional cold-plunge systems. Because purchases can exceed $50,000, the important questions are space, utilities, installation cost, lead time, and resale value — not only the advertised sauna price.

Prices checked November 10, 2026. Availability and lead times can change.

What is Sweaty Yeti?

Sweaty Yeti sells outdoor saunas designed for residential use. Its published catalog includes both traditional wood-burning and electric configurations.

The company also sells cold-plunge systems that can be paired with a sauna. Pricing for these combinations is substantially higher than for a sauna alone.

The company provides design guidance and installation support, but the buyer remains responsible for site preparation, electrical work, plumbing, and building permits where required.

Who is a Sweaty Yeti sauna for?

This product is intended for homeowners who:

  • Want a permanent outdoor sauna installation.
  • Have sufficient space for a standalone sauna structure.
  • Can support the site-preparation and installation costs.
  • Are prepared for a long planning and construction process.

It is not an entry-level sauna option. Buyers looking for a small indoor unit or a lower-cost heat-only alternative should compare significantly cheaper models first.

Cold plunge: what it adds

A cold plunge is a tub of chilled water used after a sauna session.

Sweaty Yeti's cold-plunge option increases the advertised price substantially and adds installation requirements.

The exact capacity, temperature controls, power requirements, filtration system, and maintenance schedule depend on the configuration selected.

Buyers should confirm:

  • Water supply and drainage.
  • Power requirements for chilling and filtration.
  • Local plumbing and electrical codes.
  • Cleaning and seasonal maintenance.

No health outcomes are promised here. Anyone considering sauna or cold-water immersion should consult a medical professional first.

Electricity, installation, and siting

Before ordering, buyers should confirm:

  • Electric service capacity and the cost of adding or upgrading a circuit.
  • Foundation requirements for the sauna structure.
  • Access for delivery and installation.
  • Local planning or building permits.
  • Drainage and water supply requirements for a plunge system.
  • Ventilation, insulation, and weather protection.

A site visit or contractor consultation may be necessary before a buyer commits to a configuration.

Cost, financing, and resale

The advertised product price does not include:

  • Installation labor
  • Electrical work
  • Site preparation
  • Plumbing
  • Permits
  • Delivery charges

Sweaty Yeti's website allows buyers to select a product and proceed toward checkout. Financing or deposit arrangements may apply.

Whether a financed or deposit-based purchase earns the same $1,500 flat affiliate payout as a full purchase is to be confirmed. The affiliate program states a 30-day cookie and a flat $1,500 payout per qualifying sale, according to the program's published terms.

What to check before buying

  1. Confirm the exact configuration, including heater type and plunge system.
  2. Obtain a written installed-cost estimate, not only the advertised product price.
  3. Confirm the delivery and installation timeline.
  4. Verify electrical service capacity.
  5. Confirm water supply, drainage, and plumbing requirements.
  6. Identify local permits or restrictions.
  7. Confirm the warranty period and what it covers.
  8. Ask which parts, repairs, and accessories are included.
  9. Confirm the deposit, cancellation, and refund terms.
  10. Determine how a custom installation affects resale value.
